An app for browsing Modrinth on your Android phone.

<b>Features</b>

- <b>Discover</b>: Browse through every project on Modrinth with ease.
- <b>Search</b>: Search with ease with the filtering options.
- <b>Download</b>: Download projects from mods to modpacks.
- <b>Dependency Resolution</b>: Download dependencies for project or even entire modpacks.
